# The Spindle name and logo

Spindle's code is open source under the AGPL, but the name **Spindle** and its logo are not. They belong to
Threennovate d.o.o. and tell people that something comes from us. These rules keep that meaning, while leaving you free
to do anything the AGPL allows with the code.

## What you can do

- **Say what you use.** "We track our time with Spindle", "a self-hosted Spindle server", "an integration for Spindle".
- **Run Spindle unchanged** under its name, for yourself, your team or your company, including on your own server.
- **Say your work is based on Spindle.** A fork can say "based on Spindle" or "a fork of Spindle" in its description.
- **Write about it**: articles, talks, tutorials and screenshots, using the name and the logo to refer to Spindle.

## What needs our permission

- **Calling a modified version Spindle**, or giving it a name or logo that could be mistaken for ours. A fork needs its
  own name and logo, and its apps their own icons and bundle IDs.
- **Offering a hosted service under the Spindle name**, or a name that suggests it is Spindle Cloud or run by us.
- **Using the logo in your own product or brand**, on merchandise, or in a way that suggests we endorse you.
- **Domain names, app store listings or social accounts** with "Spindle" that could be taken for ours.

If you're not sure, ask us at hello@threennovate.com. We say yes to most things that don't confuse people.

## The files

The brand files in [branding/](branding/) (the mark, the logos, the icons and the launch video) and the icons and logos
built from them into the apps are covered by this page, not the AGPL. You may use them to refer to Spindle, as above.
The fonts in `branding/fonts` keep their own license, the SIL Open Font License.
