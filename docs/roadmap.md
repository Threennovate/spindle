# Roadmap

What we plan to build next, without dates. Why Spindle works the way it does is in [principles.md](principles.md),
and what it won't do (yet) is under "Not now" there.

## Next

- **Self-hosting made easy.** A `docker-compose.yml` with the app and PostgreSQL, versioned images, and a complete
  [self-hosting guide](self-hosting.md): settings, upgrades, backups, and building the Apple apps for your own team.
- **Your own server in the iPhone app**, as the Mac, Windows and Android apps already allow.

## Later

- **Suggested descriptions.** A short description for an entry, drafted from what you were doing during it: session
  titles, branch names and the commits made in that time. A suggestion you accept or change, never a change to times,
  projects or splits.
- **Integrations** with calendars and issue trackers.
- **Invoicing.** The model is ready for it (billable and internal work, rates, agreements), but it comes after the
  tracker itself.
